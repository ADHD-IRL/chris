About Chris

Common-Sense Leadership Rooted in Service

Christopher Leiphart is a proud veteran of the United States Armed Forces and a dedicated father based in Panama City, Northwest Florida. His lived experience — in uniform and in the community — drives a no-nonsense commitment to protecting the values that matter most to Florida families.

Guided by faith and a deep respect for the Constitution, Chris believes in putting families first, holding government accountable, and ensuring every Florida citizen has a voice. He is running because he believes leadership should be transparent, principled, and close to the people it serves.
