About Christopher

Veteran. Father.
Florida Leader.

Christopher Leiphart is a proud U.S. military veteran and dedicated father who has spent his life in service — first to his country in uniform, and now to the families and communities of Northwest Florida.

Raised with a deep reverence for the Constitution and the principles that make America exceptional, Chris believes that government works best when it is transparent, accountable, and limited in its reach into the lives of everyday citizens.

As a father, Chris understands firsthand the concerns of parents who feel they are losing their voice in the education and upbringing of their children. As a veteran, he has witnessed the failures of government bureaucracy and is committed to real, meaningful reform for those who served.

His campaign is built on four pillars: protecting constitutional liberties, empowering parents, reforming the judiciary, and demanding transparent, accountable government at every level.

Transparency & Accountability

Florida Legal

Christopher Leiphart is committed to government transparency and the full exercise of citizens' rights under Florida law. Every Floridian has the right to access public records, understand how their government operates, and hold elected officials accountable.

Florida's Government in the Sunshine Law and the Public Records Act are cornerstones of democratic accountability. Chris supports robust enforcement of these laws and will fight to ensure that no government agency can hide behind bureaucratic obstruction when citizens demand the truth.

The documents and resources linked here are provided in the public interest. If you have questions about your rights or need assistance submitting a public records request, please do not hesitate to reach out to our campaign.
